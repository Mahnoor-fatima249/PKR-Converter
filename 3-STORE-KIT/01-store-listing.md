## Full description (English)

PKR Currency Converter is built around the Pakistani rupee. Open it, type an amount and the result is there straight away. It works without internet by using the last rates it saved, and it switches to live rates when you are online.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Convert between PKR and 14 other currencies: USD, EUR, GBP, AED, SAR, QAR, KWD, CNY, INR, CAD, AUD, JPY, TRY and MYR
• See a small chart that shows whether a rate is rising or falling, with 7, 30 and 90 day views
• Get a plain note on whether today's rate is above or below the 30-day average (it only looks back, it is not a forecast)
• Set rate alerts, for example "tell me when USD goes above 290" or "when SAR rises by 2%"
• Show the current rate on the toolbar icon, or get a daily summary at 9:00 am
• Select a price on any web page (like $50 or Rs 2,000), right-click and convert it
• Work out what a remittance becomes after fees, and compare with your own money changer's rate
• Gold and silver prices in PKR, a zakat calculator and a Hajj / Umrah budget planner
• English and Urdu, light and dark mode, six colour themes (or choose your own)
• Export your data, make a backup, or move your settings to another computer through your browser account

PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your settings and history stay in your own browser.

PLEASE NOTE
Rates come from free public services and are a guide only. They are not bank rates, and open-market rates in Pakistan can differ. Gold and zakat results are estimates. For your own situation, ask a scholar.
