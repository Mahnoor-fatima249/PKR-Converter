Privacy Policy

PKR Currency Converter (browser extension) · Last updated: November 16, 2026

The short version: the extension has no account, no ads and no tracking. What you type and save stays in your own browser. I do not receive, collect or sell any of it.

What is stored on your device

The extension saves the following in your browser's local extension storage. It never leaves your device unless you choose to export or sync it (see below).

  • Your settings: language, theme and colour, display options, the open-market adjustment and your own money changer rate, if you enter them.
  • Favourite currency pairs, rate alerts, and your last 10 conversions.
  • Exchange rates it has downloaded, and a daily history of them (up to about 400 days).
  • The last gold and silver price it fetched, and the numbers you type into the Hajj / Umrah budget.

Connections the extension makes

To work, the extension downloads numbers (prices and rates) from these public services. It does not send your settings, amounts or any identifier to them. Like any website, these services can see your IP address and basic request details, and their own policies apply.

open.er-api.comCurrent exchange ratesWhen you open the popup or dashboard while online, and about every 30 minutes in the background
cdn.jsdelivr.net and currency-api.pages.devPast daily rates for the trend chartWhen the saved history is short and you are online

Things that stay local

  • Right-click conversion: the text you select is read on your device to find a price. It is not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Notifications: alerts and the daily summary are created on your device.
  • Export and backup: CSV and backup files are created on your computer only when you press the button.

Sync through your browser account

If you press "Save to my Google account" (or the equivalent in your browser), your settings, favourites and alerts are copied to your browser's sync storage. That storage is run by your browser vendor under their privacy policy. Nothing is synced unless you press the button.

What I do not do

  • No analytics, advertising, cookies or tracking.
  • No accounts or logins.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because I do not have any.
  • No remote code: the extension only downloads data, never scripts.

Deleting your data

Use the Clear button in the popup to remove your recent conversions. Removing the extension deletes everything it stored on your device.

Children

The extension is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Rates are a guide

Rates, gold and silver prices, and the zakat and Hajj calculators are for information only. They are not bank quotes, financial advice or religious rulings.
